Amazing Spectacular Carbonara!

Description

Pip's Carbonara is an excellent italian dish made best with parma ham or pancetta, eggs, pecorino Romano cheese, spaghetti pasta, and a sprinkle of black pepper. Italians don't add extra ingredients. Try this authentic recipe if you want to be just like Pip!

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Spaghetti Pasta
  • Parma Ham or Pancetta
  • pecorino Romano Cheese
  • minced Garlic
  • Pan of water
  • Salt
  • Butter

Steps

  1. First boil your pan of water and add some salt for the pasta.
  2. Add some butter to a frying pan and cook on low heat.
  3. Add the minced garlic and Parma Ham/Pancetta and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Separate the yolks from the egg whites "you can save the egg whites for an egg fried rice later!" and mix the yolks together with the pecorino Romano cheese.
  5. Cook the spaghetti pasta in the boiling water.
  6. Once the parma ham/pancetta is cooked, turn the heat off and wait for the pasta to finish cooking.
  7. Strain the pasta but keep the pasta water.
  8. Add the pasta to the frying pan and add some pasta water over low heat.
  9. Cook for a few more minutes and voila, You're finished!.
